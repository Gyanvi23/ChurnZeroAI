# 🧠 ChurnZero AI – Banking Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Platform

ChurnZero AI is an AI-powered Banking Customer Churn Prediction & Retention System developed to identify customers likely to leave the bank and provide intelligent retention insights.

The platform integrates Machine Learning, Customer Intelligence, Analytics Dashboard, Recommendation Engine and AI interaction to help organizations reduce customer churn and improve retention strategies.

---

# 🚀 Project Objective

The objective of ChurnZero AI is to:

✔ Predict customer churn probability  
✔ Identify high-risk customers  
✔ Monitor customer engagement and behavior  
✔ Generate retention recommendations  
✔ Estimate business impact and revenue loss  
✔ Provide intelligent customer insights
